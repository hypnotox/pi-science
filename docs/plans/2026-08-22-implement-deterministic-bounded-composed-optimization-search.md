# Plan: Implement deterministic bounded composed optimization search

## Goal

Let ordinary advice and direct optimization discover, explain, and replay deterministic monotonic compositions of up to two shipped transformation steps. Preserve the exact objective, candidate language, ordinary analysis, failure, and no-runtime boundaries; do not add families, configurable search, nonmonotonic rewrites, or optimality claims.

## Architecture summary

Keep all search policy in Python around the existing complete-candidate verification seam. Represent each frontier item as a complete ordinary-analysis request plus retained replay analysis, selected-objective value, canonical semantic key, depth, generated-name provenance, and a bounded trace. Refactor the shipped generators into stable family lanes; breadth-first round-robin scheduling expands the unreturned root and then canonical depth-one parents, with per-depth allowances and whole-request ceilings. A transition must replay and prove against its parent with positive selected-objective savings. A retained final reuses that replay but receives an additional original-to-final proof and objective comparison.

Canonical state serialization covers the complete candidate context and capture-avoidably normalizes binders, generated producers, and equation order for search and deduplication only. Returned candidates preserve caller-facing order and retain candidate-only JSON identity. Canonical transition ordering and representative selection precede expansion so traversal, family registration, alpha-renaming, equation order, and duplicate arrival cannot select different logical populations under the same budgets.

Protocol v15 replaces the single-family suggestion projection with one- or two-step replayable traces. Every step carries local transformation, proof, objective, and complete post-step candidate evidence; plan evidence remains original-to-final. Python, generated schema, adapter, and Pi validation migrate atomically. Pi correlates request, parent, child, final, objective, search, and projection fields and presents them without applying edits or recomputing policy. `max_plans` selects only the final ranked prefix. Search exhaustion remains distinct from post-search byte truncation, and bounded output handling preserves both diagnostic classes.

The enabling family-lane and canonical-state refactor lands with its production search consumer in this one coherent protocol transaction. No preparatory refactor or general rewrite abstraction is introduced.

## Definition of done

- `dod: composed-search` A known request returns a monotonic two-step plan that neither one-step search alone can produce, while one-step results remain valid one-step traces and the root is never returned.
- `dod: canonical-fairness` Canonical complete-state deduplication and fair stable lanes make logical populations and ranking invariant to alpha-renaming, caller equation order, family registration, generator emission, duplicate arrival, and process execution within fixed budgets, while returned candidates preserve caller order.
- `dod: original-final-proof` Every trace step replays and proves against its parent with positive selected-objective savings, and every retained final independently proves and compares directly against the original; mutations at either layer are rejected.
- `dod: bounded-status` Fixed per-depth and whole-request resources produce typed search-incomplete qualifications without losing accepted plans, `max_plans` only projects ranked prefixes, and post-search byte truncation is separately qualified without changing search status.
- `dod: protocol-v15-transport` Python, schema, adapter, strict TypeScript validation, and Pi presentation agree on replayable one- or two-step protocol-v15 traces and correlations, while Pi never applies transformations or recomputes search, proof, objective, or ranking policy.
- `dod: current-documentation` All four ADR operations are applied, active guidance describes the shipped composed-search boundary consistently, generated prose is semantically reviewed, and historical records remain unchanged.
