The product boundary separates symbolic mathematical analysis from empirical and implementation-execution workflows.

## Claims

### `rule: symbolic-analysis-only`
pi-science analyzes agent-formulated symbolic or formula-oriented computation and reports abstract mathematical metrics, normalized interpretation, structure, scaling, bounds, repeated-expression extraction diagnostics, explicitly requested bounded mathematical queries, and unresolved quantities. Direct Python and Pi report bounded aggregate-work term dominance on one declared axis. It does not evaluate a submitted formula to produce the value that formula represents. Query-derived candidates are informational rather than replacement implementations or work claims. Python and Pi candidate comparison is available for exactly two explicitly mapped mathematical candidates under ADR-0003 aggregate abstract-work semantics; it establishes mapped semantics before a qualified work relation. Ordinary analysis also carries bounded informational optimization advice: Python alone proves positive whole-computation abstract-work reductions, including compatible cross-equation sharing and bounded Horner reformulation, and Pi transports the qualified result. Broader rewrite search remains future work. Direct optimization returns bounded replayable complete candidates; it remains symbolic analysis, not source editing, runtime prediction, or numerical optimization. Dataset statistics, source-to-model inference, experiment orchestration, implementation benchmarking, and physical validation remain outside the core analyzer. Formula-to-code remains an open future direction outside the current formula package scope.
