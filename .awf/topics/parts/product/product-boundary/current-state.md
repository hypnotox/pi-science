The product boundary separates symbolic mathematical analysis from empirical and implementation-execution workflows.

## Claims

### `rule: symbolic-analysis-only`
pi-science analyzes agent-formulated symbolic or formula-oriented computation and reports abstract mathematical metrics, normalized interpretation, structure, scaling, bounds, improvement opportunities, explicitly requested bounded mathematical queries, and unresolved quantities. It does not evaluate a submitted formula to produce the value that formula represents. Query-derived candidates are informational rather than replacement implementations or work claims. Dataset statistics, source-to-model inference, experiment orchestration, implementation benchmarking, and physical validation remain outside the core analyzer. Formula-to-code remains an open future direction outside the current formula package scope.
Origin: ADR-0001
