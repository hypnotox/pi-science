[Vision](vision.md) owns the product boundary, [Analysis Model](analysis-model.md) owns request and report semantics, and [Roadmap](roadmap.md) owns uncommitted expansion work.

Canonical state selection is search-only, so returned candidates preserve caller order and policy-free identity. Protocol v16 transports complete one- or two-step traces, original-to-final evidence, separate search/projection qualifications, and typed direct failures; Pi strictly correlates and presents them without deriving sums or owning mathematical policy. Ineligible proposals stay silent, and exact-symbolic replacement makes no runtime or finite-precision claim.
